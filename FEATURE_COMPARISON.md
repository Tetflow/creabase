## ❌ MISSING FEATURES (vs Fiverr/Upwork)

### 1. **Gig/Service Packages** (Fiverr Model)
**What's missing:**
- Creators can't create "gigs" with predefined packages
- No Basic/Standard/Premium tier pricing
- No "Add-ons" or extras
- No delivery time options (24h, 3 days, 7 days)
- No revision count specification

**How Fiverr does it:**
```
Gig: "I will create Instagram reels"
  - Basic: ₹2,000 (1 reel, 2 days, 1 revision)
  - Standard: ₹4,000 (3 reels, 4 days, 2 revisions)
  - Premium: ₹8,000 (5 reels, 7 days, unlimited revisions)
  - Add-ons: Script writing (+₹500), Voice over (+₹1,000)
```

## 📈 COMPETITIVE POSITIONING

**Creabase Niche: "The Fiverr for Instagram & YouTube Creators"**

**Competitive Advantages:**
1. ✅ Lower fees (11.8% vs 20%)
2. ✅ Social media verification
3. ✅ India-focused (language, location, currency)
4. ✅ Influencer-specific (not general freelancing)

**Weaknesses to Address:**
1. ❌ Fewer features than Fiverr/Upwork
2. ❌ No mobile apps yet
3. ❌ Limited payment methods
4. ❌ No gig packages

**Strategy:**
- Start niche (content creators only)
- Add features iteratively
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Build trust through verification
- Keep fees low (competitive advantage)
